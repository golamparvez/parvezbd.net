A little about me

Curiosity first. Progress follows.

A people-focused educator with a practical, encouraging approach to learning and student support.

I work across mathematics education, private tutoring, and student counselling. Over five years of private tuition, I’ve supported learners from Class 3 through first-year honours, with a focus on making complex subjects feel approachable.

Alongside teaching, I currently counsel students exploring higher education abroad and support them with university applications. I also bring hands-on digital skills in graphic design, digital marketing, and online work.

I’m currently pursuing a B.Sc. in Mathematics at National University. I value clear communication, steady effort, and helping people take the next step with confidence.
